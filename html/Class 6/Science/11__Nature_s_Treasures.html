Topic 11.1

11.1 Air around Us

We can survive without food or water for days, but cannot survive without oxygen for even a few minutes!

Composition of Air (10x10 Grid)

Nitrogen: 78%

Oxygen: 21%

Argon, CO2, others: 1%

Note: Moving air is called wind. We can see its presence when leaves rustle or paper pinwheels (firki) rotate!

Windmills

Wind Power

Windmills operate on the same principle as a paper firki. They are used to:

  • Run flour mills
  • Pump underground water
  • Generate clean electricity

Leading Wind Farms in India:

  • • Muppandal Wind Farm - Tamil Nadu
  • • Jaisalmer Wind Park - Rajasthan
  • • Brahmanvel Wind Farm - Maharashtra
11.2 Water

11.2 Water Wisdom

~2/3 of Earth is covered by water!

🌊 Saline Water: Found in oceans & seas. Unfit for domestic, industrial, or farming use.

❄️ Freshwater: Locked in ice sheets, snow, rivers, lakes, and underground. Extremely precious!

11.3 Sun

11.3 Solar Energy

The Sun is the ultimate source of energy on Earth. All plants and animals depend directly or indirectly on it.

How is Solar Energy Captured?

  • ☀️ Direct Heat: Solar cookers, drying clothes & crops (e.g., chillies, mangoes).
  • ⚡ Solar Panels: Capture sunlight & convert it into electricity for households, street lights, and traffic signals.
💚 "Plants prepare food via photosynthesis using sunlight. Animals eat plants. Thus, even a grass-eating cow depends on the Sun!"
11.4 Forests

11.4 Forests & Nature

Forests are large areas with dense growth of plants providing food and shelter to wildlife.

🌱

Van Mahotsav

A week-long tree plantation festival celebrated across India in July.

🤝

Chipko Movement

Started in the early 1970s in Uttarakhand. Brave local women hugged trees to prevent them from being felled!

Traditions forbid plucking certain fruits; they are left entirely for forest animals and birds!

11.5 Soil

11.5 Soil & Rocks

Soil Formation: Formed by the disintegration (breaking apart) of rocks via the actions of the Sun, water, wind, and organisms over thousands of years!

Earthworms are natural tillers!

They help loosen the soil, creating air spaces for plant roots to grow smoothly.

Rocks Used

Slate (roofing), laterite (bricks), granite, sandstone, marble (sculptures/construction).

Minerals Extracted

Aluminium, Gold, Copper, Iron, Silver, Cobalt (vital for mobile phones!).

11.6 Fuels

11.6 Fossil Fuels

Formed from the remains of microorganisms and plants buried deep inside the Earth over millions of years.

🪨 Coal Electricity production & factories
⛽ Petrol & Diesel Extracted from petroleum
💨 Natural Gas / CNG Clean fuel for cooking & vehicles
💡 CNG & LPG: Modern eco-friendly alternatives that have gradually replaced highly polluting fuels like coal, wood, and dung cakes in households.
11.7 Classes

11.7 Natural Resources

Renewable

Get renewed or restored by natural processes within a short span of time.

☀️ Air, Water, Forest

Non-Renewable

Exist in limited quantities and take millions of years to form.

🛢️ Coal, Petrol, Minerals

🤖 Human-made resources: Built by humans for convenience (e.g., electric bulbs, bicycles, solar panels, furniture).
