1. Biodiversity & Grouping

Biodiversity: The variety of plants and animals found in a particular region.

Why Group Organisms?

Grouping makes it easier to study plants & animals based on their similarities and differences.

  • Plants & animals depend on each other for food, shelter, seed dispersal & survival!
  • Criteria for grouping: Height, stem, leaves, flowers, seeds, habitat, movement.

2. Plant Types (Stem & Height)

🌿 Herbs Short

Soft & tender green stem; thin & easy to bend.

e.g., Tomato, Grass, Mustard

🪴 Shrubs Medium

Hard, thin woody stem; branches arise close to ground.

e.g., Rose, Hibiscus, Tulsi

🌳 Trees Tall

Hard, thick & brown woody stem (trunk); branches start high up.

e.g., Mango, Neem, Deodar

Climbers: Weak stem, needs support to climb (e.g., Pea).
Creepers: Spread horizontally on ground (e.g., Pumpkin).

3. Leaf Venation & Roots

Pattern of veins = Venation

🕸️ Reticulate Venation

Net-like vein pattern on both sides of main vein.

Leaves: Hibiscus, Rose, Sadabahar

║ Parallel Venation

Veins run parallel to each other.

Leaves: Banana, Grass, Lemongrass

🥕 Taproot System

1 main primary root with smaller lateral side roots.

Roots: Mustard, Chickpea (Chana)

🌾 Fibrous Root System

Bunch of thin, equal-sized roots from stem base.

Roots: Grass, Wheat, Maize

🔥 THE ULTIMATE PLANT MATCHING RULE 🔥
DICOT = 2 Cotyledons
→ Reticulate + Taproot
MONOCOT = 1 Cotyledon
→ Parallel + Fibrous Root

4. Animal Grouping & Movements

Animals are grouped based on movement type, body parts used, habitat & eating habits.

Animal Movement Body Parts Used
Fish Swims Fins & Streamlined body
Goat / Cow Walks / Runs Legs & Hooves
Pigeon / Bird Flies / Walks Wings & Legs
Frog Jumps / Swims Hind legs (Amphibian!)
🐸 Amphibians: Animals that can live comfortably both in water and on land (e.g., Frogs).

5. Habitat & Adaptations (Desert vs Mountain)

Habitat: The place where plants/animals live (provides food, water, air, shelter).
Adaptations: Special features that enable organisms to survive in their surroundings.

Hot Desert Camel (Rajasthan)

  • Long legs & wide hooves: Walk on hot sand without sinking or gaining ground heat.
  • 1 Hump: Stores fat for food scarcity.
  • Water Conservation: Excretes tiny urine, dry dung, does NOT sweat!

Cold Desert Camel (Ladakh)

  • Shorter legs: Walk easily in rocky/mountainous terrain.
  • 2 Humps: Shrink in late winter as stored fat is consumed.
  • Long hair/fur: Thick coat protects from extreme cold.
🌵 Cactus (Desert Plant): Thick fleshy stems store water; green stem does photosynthesis.
🌲 Deodar Tree (Mountains): Conical shape + sloping branches let heavy snow slide off easily.

6. Famous Indian Scientists

  • Documented & preserved India's rich plant biodiversity.
  • Key leader in 'Save Silent Valley' movement.
  • Head of the Botanical Survey of India (BSI).
🦅 Dr. Salim Ali 'Birdman of India'
  • Documented bird species, habitats & travel routes across India.
  • Saved Keoladeo National Park (Bharatpur) & Ranganathittu Sanctuary.
  • Awarded Padma Vibhushan (1976).

7. Conservation Efforts

🌳 Sacred Groves: Undisturbed forest patches protected traditionally by local communities. Home to rare & medicinal plants! No harm is allowed.
🏞️ Save Silent Valley Movement: 10-year people's battle in Palakkad, Kerala to save a pristine moist evergreen forest from a hydroelectric dam project.
Key Government Projects:
  • Project Tiger (1973): Protect declining Bengal Tigers.
  • Cheetah Reintroduction (2022): Restore Cheetahs in India.
  • Great Indian Bustard: Declared protected areas in GJ, RJ, MH.

8. Quick Revision Formula

Habitat = Food + Water + Air + Shelter
Habitat Types:
  • Terrestrial: Land (Forest, Desert, Mountain)
  • Aquatic: Water (Pond, Lake, River, Ocean)
⚠️ WARNING: Habitat Loss = Loss of Biodiversity!
