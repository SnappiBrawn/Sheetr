Science = Observing + Wondering + Doing!

The Scientific Method (Step-by-Step)

5 STEPS

Science is not just memorizing facts — it is a systematic process to solve mysteries:

1. Observe Notice something interesting or odd.
2. Question Ask why or how it happens.
3. Guess Form a possible answer (Hypothesis).
4. Test Perform experiments or observation.
5. Analyze Check if results answer the question!
Example: "Why did my pen stop writing?"

• Observation: Pen stopped writing. → Guess 1: Ink is finished.

• Test 1: Open pen & check refill. Refill is full! Guess 1 was wrong!

• Guess 2: Ink has dried at the tip. → Test 2: Scribble on rough paper or warm tip → It works!

Everyday Scientists

You don't need a lab coat! Anyone using the scientific method is working like a scientist:

  • The Cook: "Why did the dal spill out of the pressure cooker? Was there too much water?"
  • Cycle Mechanic: "Why is the tyre flat? Where is the air leaking from?"
  • Electrician: "Why is the light bulb not working? Is it the bulb or the switch?"

What We Explore

NATURE Living World: Seed → Plant growth, Caterpillar → Butterfly, Animal life.
FOOD Food & Nutrition: Tasty cuisines across India & what materials make them up.
PHYSICS Water & Temperature: Rain, freezing → ice, boiling → steam, hot vs. cold, fever.
MATTER Materials Around Us: Paper, metal key, plastic ruler, rubber eraser, magnets & separation.
SPACE Beyond Earth: Sun, Moon, Stars, Galaxies, Deserts, Oceans & Mountains.

Golden Rules of Science

To be a wise person, you must be a "WHYS" person!

  • Teamwork: Science is rarely done alone! Scientists across the world work together in large teams.
  • Long Journey: You won't find all answers in Grade 6. It is a 5-year journey and beyond!
  • Never Stop Wondering: Keep exploring, keep asking questions, and enjoy the adventure!
