2. Energy & Body Building

Sec 3.2

Carbohydrates

Energy-Giving

Primary source of immediate energy (e.g., Glucose for runners).

Sources: Rice, Wheat, Potato, Sweet Potato, Banana, Maize, Sugarcane.

Fats

Stored Energy

Provides reserve energy & keeps body warm (e.g. Laddoos in winter).

Sources: Ghee, Butter, Nuts (Almonds, Walnuts), Seeds, Oils, Cream.
Polar Bears: Store thick fat layer under skin for months-long winter hibernation!

Proteins

Body-Building

Essential for growth, muscle repair & tissue development.

Plant: Pulses, Beans, Peas, Soya bean, Mushrooms.
Animal: Milk, Paneer, Fish, Eggs, Meat.

3. Protective Nutrients

Sec 3.2

Required in small amounts to protect body against diseases and maintain health.

Nutrient Main Function Deficiency
Vit A Healthy eyes & skin Night Blindness
Vit B1 Heart & muscle support Beriberi
Vit C Fights diseases, wound healing Scurvy (Bleeding gums)
Vit D Calcium absorption for bones Rickets (Soft bones)
Calcium Strong bones & teeth Bone/Tooth decay
Iodine Physical/mental growth Goitre (Neck swelling)
Iron Blood component (Haemoglobin) Anaemia (Weakness)
Case 1 (1746): James Lind found citrus fruits (lemons/oranges) cured sailors' Scurvy.
Case 2 (1960s): Himalayan neck swelling cured by fortifying salt with iodine → Iodised Salt.

Vit D made naturally in body via sunlight!

4. Food Testing Lab Cheat

Sec 3.3
1. Starch Test Dilute Iodine Solution

Food + 2-3 drops Dilute Iodine Solution

Positive Result → Turns BLUE-BLACK
2. Fat Test Paper Press

Wrap food in paper & crush gently (dry if wet).

Positive Result → OILY TRANSLUCENT PATCH
3. Protein Test CuSO4 + Caustic Soda

Food Paste + 2 drops Copper Sulphate + 10 drops Caustic Soda

Positive Result → Turns VIOLET / PURPLE
Safety Precaution: Chemicals are harmful! Do not touch skin, taste or smell reagents. Wash immediately if spilled.

5. Roughage, Water & Diet

Sec 3.4

Roughage (Fibre)

  • No nutrients directly
  • Helps bowel movement & prevents constipation
  • Sources: Fruits, veggies, whole grains, pulses

Water

  • Absorbs nutrients
  • Flushes waste via sweat & urine
  • Essential for body balance

What is a Balanced Diet?

A diet containing ALL essential nutrients (carbs, fats, proteins, vitamins, minerals) in the right proportion, along with roughage & water.

6. Millets & Food Miles

Sec 3.5 - 3.6

Millets (Nutri-Cereals)

Indigenous small grains, easy to cultivate in diverse climates, highly nutritious!

Jowar Bajra Ragi Sanwa (Barnyard)

Food Miles: Farm to Plate

Distance food travels from producer to consumer.

1. Farm Growth → 2. Threshing/Winnowing
→ 3. Storage & Grinding → 4. Transport to Market
Why reduce food miles? Reduces transport cost & pollution, supports local farmers, keeps food fresh & healthy!
