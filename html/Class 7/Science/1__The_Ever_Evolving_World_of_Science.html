The Scientific Mindset

Science is not just a heavy textbook filled with dusty facts! It is a dynamic process of thinking that welcomes curiosity, asks deeper questions, and explores the unknown.

3 Core Questions to Live By:

  • How do things around us work?
  • Why do natural events happen?
  • What patterns can we find in nature?
💡 Rule #1: To be a wise person, you must be a "whys person"!

How We Measure Time

Time tells us when to wake up, go to school, and sleep. But how did humans measure speed and time before digital wristwatches existed?

1

The Shadow Age

Early humans watched shadows move with the Sun. Shorter/longer shadows dictated the hourly flow.

2

The Sundial Evolution

Precision plates marked with hour lines, relying entirely on direct sun angles.

3

Modern Mechanics

Pendulum clocks, spring-loaded gear watches, and ultra-precise digital quartz oscillators.
